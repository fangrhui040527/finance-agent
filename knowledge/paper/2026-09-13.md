## Lessons proposed

- New information can argue for less action. Both candidate names got weaker
  when the measurement arrived, which is the decomposition doing its job — and
  a book that only records what it bought would show none of it.
- A verdict is only as good as the thing it is measured against. "Significant
  idiosyncratic move" against a proxy that did not really trade is a statement
  about one instrument's history wearing the clothes of a market comparison.
- Judge a move against the name's own volatility before calling it large. Two
  pages called a 5.6% session extraordinary in a name for which that is 1.45
  sigma, and the real anomaly — a 19% week — was sitting beside it unlabelled.
