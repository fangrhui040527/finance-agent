## Lessons proposed

- A book weighted on closes whose session is in doubt inherits that doubt at full
  size; the cheapest response to a dating fault is to hold nothing until it is
  settled.
- A high round-trip cost is a hurdle the thesis must clear before the position
  starts, not a friction to net off at the end: at 4.35% a 21-session view has to
  be right by more than 4.35% to have been worth taking.
