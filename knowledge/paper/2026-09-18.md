## Lessons proposed

- An announced event resolves on the day its outcome is collected, not on the day it convenes. The review met on 09-17 and produced nothing collected; the figure arrived on 09-18, and a page that had closed the question a day early would have closed it on air.
- A figure for a name over the cap is still a figure. RM120-150 million is the first number this journal holds for Tenaga's side of the AFA, and it is recorded in full even though no weight can follow from it.
- A collected 'why' for the market is not a collected 'why' for a name. 'Bursa rebounds on lower oil prices' is a market wrap; it neither explains Petronas Chemicals' fall nor can it be checked against a crude print the fact book does not have.
- A thesis is checked against the rules before it is recorded, not after. 'The last observe decision' was written from a calendar in the head instead of phase_for, and the store now carries the error permanently.
- A phase change widens what the book may hold, not what it has evidence for. Ramp raises the invested cap from 0% to 40% on Tuesday and admits no name that tonight's page refuses.
