## Lessons proposed

- A weekend decision is a row, not a session, and it says so. Two of the three rows recorded since Friday's close grade against the same control on consecutive days; the record is one decision a night because the contract asks for one, and the page names which ones are calendar rows.
- The fundable table can be three pages old and still current. Thursday's closes at Thursday's rate stand until a market opens; the date on the table, not the date on the page, is what the reader checks.
- The last observe session is the last free look. Monday's page is the final one whose refusals cost nothing; from Tuesday a refusal is a position not taken, and the reasons carried into it are the ones written this weekend.
