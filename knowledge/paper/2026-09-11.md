## Lessons proposed

- A cap that is never breached still decides. The per-name limit refused nothing
  tonight because the trade was never proposed — the only name worth a thesis
  was unaffordable before any rule was consulted, and a book that only records
  breaches would show no constraint at work.
- An instrument's price and its evidence can move in opposite directions for
  days. Treating the price as the signal to go looking is sound; treating the
  absence of evidence as a reason to act anyway is not.
- A system that cannot record "nothing" cannot be graded on restraint. Three
  nights of correct inaction left no trace, and the fix was one row.
