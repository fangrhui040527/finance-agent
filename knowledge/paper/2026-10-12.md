## Lessons proposed

- **An announced cash call can move a name more in one session than its whole fitted distribution allows.** Genting fell 22.87% the session after the rights issue was reported; a sigma fitted on ordinary days says nothing about a day the share count changes.
- **A passive control that holds the affected name makes every all-cash claim look right that day.** Grades against the control after 10-12 carry Genting's fall; read them as the control's loss, not as a finding about the names.
