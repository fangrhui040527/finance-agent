## Lessons proposed

- **A day return is only as good as the previous day's last mark.** When a later mark of the same day is written from worse data, every return measured from it inherits the error; the first mark of a day from settled closes is the one to read.
- **A dividend credit is a position change with no trade.** The index book's first two credits moved its cash by USD 209.25 on 10-13 with no fee, spread or slippage; reading cash changes as trading would misread them.
