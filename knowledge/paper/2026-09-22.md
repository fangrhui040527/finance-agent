## Lessons proposed

- A decision recorded after its fill bar has opened is a decision the ledger cannot audit. Whatever the reasons for the delay, the only row that stays clean is the one with no fill, and the page has to say which case it is.
- A price table is as fresh as the cache rule behind it. Tuesday's table read Monday's Bursa closes under no warning at all, while the US quotes were labelled; the stale half was the unlabelled one.
- Two clocks on one decision is a defect even when both agree on the day. The target row says 02:33 UTC on the 23rd and the prediction says 22:30 UTC on the 22nd; a grader that reads the second would place the decision before the open it fills at.
