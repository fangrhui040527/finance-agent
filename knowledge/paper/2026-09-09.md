## Lessons proposed

- A decision to hold nothing is still a decision, and a system that records only
  non-empty decisions cannot grade its own caution. Two nights in, the observe
  phase has graded nothing because nothing was written.
- When the evidence is unusable rather than absent, the honest position is the
  same as when it is absent — but the two should not read alike afterwards, or
  a fixable data fault gets filed as a quiet market.
- A falling share price raises the round-trip hurdle on a fixed-lot market: the
  ticket shrinks, the per-share and minimum costs do not.
