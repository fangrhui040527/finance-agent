## Lessons proposed

- **A decision's store is as fresh as its last sweep for that market.** The 10-09 decision read no Genting row because no Bursa sweep ran between 10:09Z and the decision. Rows published after a market's last sweep are absent, not non-existent.
- **A company event reported after the close belongs to the next session's claim, not the last one's.** Genting's rights issue cannot be graded against Friday's close; the first price on it is Monday's.
