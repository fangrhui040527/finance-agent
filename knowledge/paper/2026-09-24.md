## Lessons proposed

- A significance level is only as good as the window under it. A two-session return scored against a one-session sigma looks rarer than it is, so a decision that keys on "significant at 5%" needs the pack to state how many sessions the return spans.
- Fourteen all-cash rows are fourteen claims, not fourteen abstentions. Each is graded against a control that is now invested and 1.07% down, so the record will show whether cash was a view or a default.
- A time on a page is a figure like any other, and it is copied, not typed. Tonight's feedback page stated a written time twelve minutes ahead of its own commit.
