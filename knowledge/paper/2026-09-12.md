## Lessons proposed

- A condition can be met and change nothing. Three pages made the collector the
  reason for inaction; when it came back the book stayed in cash, which means
  the collector was never the binding constraint — only the visible one.
- An event that can be dated precisely and cannot be sized is not tradeable.
  Knowing exactly which session priced the news is worth less than knowing what
  fraction of the company the news was about.
- Price and evidence can diverge for a whole week without either being wrong.
  The correct response to a 19% move with no coverage is to widen the search,
  not the position.
