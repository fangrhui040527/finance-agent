1. What a share actually is
A share is a fractional claim on the cash a business will generate for its owners after everyone else has been paid: suppliers, staff, lenders, the tax office. The ticker is a queue of opinions about that claim, updated every second. The claim itself changes slowly, on the cadence of quarterly results and capital decisions. Keeping the two apart is the first analytical habit: the business is the asset, the price is a measurement of the crowd.

2. The test that keeps the distinction honest
If the exchange closed for five years, the holding would still be worth something, and the only way to say what is to estimate the cash the business will send its owners and what someone would pay for that stream when trading resumes. An investor who cannot answer that question owns a price, not a business. This system's fact book stores the figures that answer it, each stamped with the day it became knowable, so the answer can be reconstructed as it looked at any date.

3. Who is on the other side of the trade
Every trade has a counterparty who believes the opposite, or has a constraint that has nothing to do with belief. Three common reasons someone sells a stock they still think is cheap: a fund is meeting redemptions and must raise cash; an index has dropped the name and passive holders sell mechanically; a risk desk has hit a loss limit and is cutting exposure regardless of view. Two common reasons someone buys a stock they think is expensive: a benchmark forces the weight, or a short position is being closed. None of these counterparties is a signal about value. The market is not a person with intentions; it is a crowd with different horizons and different rules.

4. What follows for an analyst
Because the counterparty may be constrained rather than informed, a falling price is not evidence that the thesis is wrong, and a rising one is not evidence that it is right. The evidence is in the figures and the events. The decomposition engine in this system splits any move into what the market did, what the sector did and what is left over before any cause is named, for exactly this reason.
