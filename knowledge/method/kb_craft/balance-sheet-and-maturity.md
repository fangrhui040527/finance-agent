1. What is owned, what is owed, and when
The balance sheet lists what the business controls and who has a claim on it, at one instant. The misconception to kill is that debt is bad. Debt is a schedule of dates on which cash must be produced. The analytical questions are when the maturities fall, whether the cash to meet them exists or can be generated, and what happens to the terms if a covenant is breached.

2. Liquidity versus solvency
Liquidity is the ability to meet the next twelve months of obligations: current assets against current liabilities, cash against short-term debt, undrawn facilities. Solvency is whether total assets are worth more than total liabilities on a realistic view of the assets. A solvent company can fail from illiquidity if its maturities cluster; an illiquid-looking company with a committed facility may be fine. The two questions need separate answers.

3. Duration mismatch
Assets and liabilities have durations. A bank funding long-dated bonds with overnight deposits is carrying interest-rate risk on its balance sheet, and a rise in rates marks the assets down while the deposits can leave at par. Silicon Valley Bank's 2023 failure was this mismatch, visible in its filings a year before it ran. The pattern generalises: any business funding long assets with short money is one shock away from a forced sale.

4. Hidden and off-balance-sheet leverage
Operating leases (now mostly on balance sheet under IFRS 16), guarantees, receivables factoring, supplier finance, and derivatives with collateral calls are all forms of leverage that a headline debt-to-equity ratio misses. The notes are where they live. A quality-of-earnings check that stops at the face of the statements has not been done.

5. Ratios that summarise the schedule
Net debt to EBITDA for how many years of operating cash the debt represents; interest cover for how much profit is available per unit of interest; the current ratio for near-term liquidity; Altman's Z as a composite distress score with a long record. Each is computed by the ratio layer in this system from stored lines and each says which inputs it lacked.
