1. The only test that is measured
A competitive advantage is a claim; a return on invested capital above the cost of capital, sustained for years while competitors could have entered, is the measurement. Return on invested capital is operating profit after tax divided by invested capital (debt plus equity minus cash, or operating assets minus operating liabilities). The spread over the weighted average cost of capital, and the number of consecutive years it has been positive, is the moat test this system runs when the statement lines exist.

2. Five sources, and what evidence each leaves
Cost advantage shows as a gross margin above peers at the same price, or the same margin at a lower price. Intangible assets, brands, patents and licences, show as pricing power: price rises without volume loss. Network effects show as a market share that grows faster than the market and a customer acquisition cost that falls with scale. Switching costs show as retention above 90 percent and a long customer tenure. Efficient scale shows as a market with room for one or two profitable players and no new entrants for a decade. A moat claim that cannot point to one of these signatures is a story.

3. Five forces as a checklist
Threat of new entrants, bargaining power of suppliers, bargaining power of buyers, threat of substitutes, rivalry among existing competitors. Run once per name, each force rated with the evidence for the rating. The point is not the rating; it is that the exercise forces the analyst to name the buyer with the power and the substitute that could arrive.

4. Moats erode
The most common failure of a quality thesis is holding a business whose spread is shrinking while the multiple still prices the old spread. The direction of the spread matters more than its level. A five-year series of ROIC against WACC, with the trend, is the standing exhibit; a single-year figure is a snapshot that can be a peak.

5. In this system
The competitive-position step of the workup computes the spread from stored lines and the cost-of-capital table, reports how many years it has been positive, and marks the step partial or unavailable when the lines are missing. For Bursa names that today means the step names the statement source it lacks.
