## Lessons proposed

- A market that did not trade is a finding, not a blank. Six of nine names had no session today, and the page that restates yesterday's figures without saying so has invented a day of data.
- A calendar with no holidays in it is not a calendar, it is a weekday filter. Nothing broke today only because the decomposition reads cached bars rather than asking which days were sessions.
- The same source, at the same quality score, can narrate a product in both directions in twenty-four hours. Apple's two q 1.00 headlines bracket two sessions that together move less than the name's ordinary daily variation — which is the argument for running the decomposition before reading the news, stated as cleanly as this journal is likely to get it.
- A vintaged series protects the page that used it. Quoting Brent at 109.51 on Tuesday was correct on Tuesday; the 130.80 print did not exist yet, and a store that let the page silently pick up today's number would have made Tuesday's reasoning unreproducible.
- An event's sign is evidence, and so is the market's reading of it. The 09-15 page declined to name a dividend increase behind a fall; the press then named exactly that. Both can be recorded without either becoming a cause, because the decomposition still says the session was ordinary.
