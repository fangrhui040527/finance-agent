## Lessons proposed

- A gap in the proxy on the session before the one measured is as dangerous as a gap on the session itself. The first mis-dates the row, and the pack says so. The second silently stretches "1d" over two sessions and tests it against a one-session sigma. Both significant verdicts tonight come from such a window.
- A window that contains the event is not a window that isolates it. IHH's two-session return holds the whole Macquarie day and a flat Wednesday, so the share is right for the window and says nothing about how the day split between IHH and its sector.
- A story dated to the day can still be after the close. Both Tenaga rows dated 2026-09-23 were published after Bursa shut. Checking the publish time against the market's close belongs before any row is read as evidence for that day's move.
