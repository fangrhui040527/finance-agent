## Lessons proposed

- **A non-trading day is a page about the machine, not the market.** Saturday's pack re-measured Friday with a window one day longer; reading the changed shares as a second day's moves would double-count Friday.
- **A short alias matched as a word still matches inside another company's name.** The 10-10 rule links a row only where the text names the company, and *Shree TNB Polymers* names TNB. Linking by the text fixes vendor tags, not alias collisions.
