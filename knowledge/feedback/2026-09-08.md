## Lessons proposed

- A cache whose *history* changes between fetches cannot be reasoned about from
  one read; a figure taken from it is only as good as the fetch it came from,
  and the fetch date belongs beside the figure.
- The cost of a silent fallback is not constant — it scales with how much news
  the day carried. Two nights of the same fault: the first cost nothing because
  no name had a story, the second buried a ratings downgrade, a RM4.83bn
  approval and a +3.95% move.
- A defect that self-heals a day later is the hardest kind to notice and the
  easiest to keep shipping, because every retrospective check finds the data
  correct.
