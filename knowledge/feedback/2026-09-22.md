## Lessons proposed

- A blank bar shared by a name and its proxy looks like a holiday. A mis-dating check that fires only when one side printed called Tuesday's US rows *correctly dated* to Monday at 22:44Z, and a refetch two minutes later showed Tuesday traded. Only an independent print, here FRED's `SP500` for 2026-09-22, could have told the first build.
- A headline figure is not a measurement. IHH's *"over 5%"* arrived on the one session the pack could not decompose; the page can record the claim, but cannot size it, split it from the market, or call it a cause.
- Changing the estimation window changes last week's findings without a new price. The same Monday went from 11% to 30% unexplained for Tenaga when the betas moved from 120 to 260 sessions, so a comparison of shares across pages needs the window stated beside them.
