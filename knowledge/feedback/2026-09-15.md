## Lessons proposed

- A verdict that says "nothing matched" should not be readable as "nothing was there to match". `no_identified_catalyst` on a Bursa name reports an empty candidate list, and the page that quotes it without saying so is reporting a data gap as an analytical finding.
- When a component takes a list, check what is in the list before judging what it did with it. Three pages read the catalyst matcher as too strict; it was never given anything to be strict about.
- An event's sign is part of its evidence. A dividend increase on a day the name fell 1.64% is not a weak explanation, it is an explanation pointing the wrong way, and saying so costs nothing.
- Coverage that names the company is not the same as coverage about the company. Six of Maybank's rows name it as the research house issuing a call on somebody else.
- A source that fails is not a source that is gone. Both of this week's outages — `nst_business` on 404, `edgar` on 429 — recovered on the next sweep without intervention, and both were written up as worse than they were.
