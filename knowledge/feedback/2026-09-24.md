## Lessons proposed

- A story that is about the company can still be the wrong session's story. All seven IHH appeal rows landed after Bursa's close, so the check on publish time against the market's close decides which day a row can explain before anything else about it is weighed.
- A monitor that forgets what it reported re-reports it. The same NEW warning on two consecutive nights is a sign that the alert state is not being kept, not that the market changed twice.
- A shared word is not a shared company. "TNB" matched an Indian polymer IPO and "Genting" matched Ho Wah Genting. A linker rule that matches acronyms or common words needs the market or the company form (Bhd, Berhad) beside it before it attaches a row to a name.
