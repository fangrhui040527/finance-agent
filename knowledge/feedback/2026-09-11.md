## Lessons proposed

- A market proxy whose history changes between reads is not a proxy but a
  delayed one. Every figure computed against it is provisional until its own
  past stops moving, and a page that says "this could not be computed" should
  expect to be contradicted by tomorrow's fetch.
- Removing a constraint tests a diagnosis better than examining it. Three pages
  argued from log signatures that the outage was billing; one configuration
  change settled it in a day.
- Volume without a price is evidence about the vendor, not the market. An
  instrument that traded and did not print was not quiet.
- A filter placed at one seam does not clean the readers on the other side of
  it. Two counts of the same corpus now exist, and the page has to say which
  one a number came from.
