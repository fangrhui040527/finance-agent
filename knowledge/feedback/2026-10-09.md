## Lessons proposed

- **A story published after the close cannot explain that close.** Genting's rights issue rows are timed 11:18Z–11:55Z, after Bursa's 09:00Z close. They bear on the next session, and a page that matched them to Friday's +2.17% would have the order of events backwards.
- **A premarket report is measured in the open as well as the close.** Apple opened −2.56% on the Nikkei report and closed −1.11%. The close-to-close leg (1.13 sigma) shows half of the day's reaction and none of its recovery.
- **A collector's own timestamp can run ahead of the clock.** `nst_business` reported an item at 16:33Z from a sweep at 15:24Z. Any time from a source whose oldest item is later than its own sweep is a dating error until the adapter is checked.
