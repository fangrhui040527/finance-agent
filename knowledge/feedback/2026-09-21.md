## Lessons proposed

- The evidence day and the price day are different days. Tenaga's twelve rows about Friday's figure arrived on a session it did not move (+0.00%, 11% unexplained); a page that reads only what moved would never have read them, and a page that reads them as causes of Monday would be wrong.
- A guard keyed to the calendar fails in the direction of the delay. A cron that runs late crosses midnight; counted by date, the late run spends the next day's slot, so one close is collected twice and the next not at all, and a count of runs per day reports both days as complete.
- Three names falling the same amount on the same flat day is a pattern the decomposition cannot report while the sector beta is 0: each residual reads as that company's own, and each is marked `unknown` separately.
