## Lessons proposed

- A fix can work exactly as designed and still not deliver the benefit it was
  justified by. The query now asks for the ticker and the ticker is what retail
  social platforms tag posts with, not what the press prints in a headline.
  Verifying the mechanism is not verifying the claim.
- Where a number cannot be trusted, labelling beats refusing. The mis-dated rows
  still carry a real return; what they lacked was a reader who knew which day it
  belonged to.
- An aggregator going quiet is not a publisher going quiet, and one route to a
  series is not evidence about the series. A second route would have cost one
  request and would have saved a quarter-sized error in an aluminium smelter's
  input price.
- Infrastructure has a budget and the budget is a dependency. A collector that
  cannot get a runner is indistinguishable, from inside the data, from a market
  with no news.
