## Lessons proposed

- **A timezone assumed in code is a claim until a live record checks it.** EDGAR's acceptance "Z" was assumed to mean New York time and was never checked; the first results 8-K read after the change was fetched before the time the assumption gave it. A collection timestamp later than a stored "event" time is a cheap test for this kind of error.
