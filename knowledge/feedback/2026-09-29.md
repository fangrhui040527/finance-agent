## Lessons proposed

- **A headline's attribution is not a decomposition.** *Maybank Falls 1.2% After… Sukuk Redemption* described a session in which the company's own leg was 0.07 of 1.67 points. Read the market leg before the headline's cause.
- **Where the move happens decides which rows can be causes.** Apple opened −0.42% and closed −2.66%, so a note first seen 55 minutes after the open can be a candidate. NVIDIA on Monday was complete at the open, and a session-hours row could not be. Check the open before weighing the timestamps.
- **A significant leg with no row is still a finding, and the peers bound it.** Tenaga fell 2.52 sigma while YTL Power and Gas Malaysia rose. With nothing collected, the page can say it was not a utilities day. It cannot say why.
