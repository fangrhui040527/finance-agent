## Lessons proposed

- A stale number is more dangerous than a missing one. The blank proxy stopped
  the arithmetic and announced itself; the backfilled one restarted it with a
  market return of zero, which reads as a finding and is an artefact of 500
  shares.
- A rule that names its own source beats a rule that names the outage. Three
  cadence alerts fired for one missing slot, and each was independently
  actionable; a single "us_close is late" would have said less.
- An alert can close because the machine recovered rather than because anyone
  acted, and that is worth recording either way — otherwise the next reader
  cannot tell a fix from a remission.
- A story about a different listing of the same issuer is a link, not a cause,
  and an escalation gate keyed on the company name cannot tell them apart.
