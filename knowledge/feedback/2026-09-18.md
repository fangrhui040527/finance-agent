## Lessons proposed

- A verdict that never consults the evidence is describing itself. "No catalyst matched" on the one day the corpus held the catalyst means no matching was attempted; the sentence is a placeholder for a matcher, and a page that reads it as a finding has been told something false.
- The first session after an announcement is where the price answers it, not the announcement day. +0.00% on the evening the exemptions were announced, −2.10% the next session, and the figure published fifteen minutes after that close.
- A name's own sigma remembers its run. After +22.56% in five sessions, a −3.10pp idiosyncratic day is 0.78 sigma and reads *not significant*; significance is relative to the window's variance, and the window contains the anomaly it is being asked to judge.
- A market wrap's "why" is a claim about the index and is recorded as a test, not a cause. "Lower oil prices" became a Brent-print watch item, not a reason for a petrochemical name's fall.
- A carried question is checked against the store before it is carried. The capex figure the 09-16 page said did not exist had been in the fact book since 2026-07-29; three nights carried a question one lookup would have closed.
