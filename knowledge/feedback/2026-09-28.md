## Lessons proposed

- **A gap at the open limits the candidates to what was public before it.** NVIDIA opened +2.08% and closed +1.68%. Only items published before 13:30Z Monday could have moved it, and both candidates were. A session-hours row cannot be a cause of a move that was complete at the open.
- **A claim can be priced by the counterparty and not by the defendant.** The USD 5.7 billion verdict moved Burford Capital over 12% and Apple 0.21 sigma. For a large defendant, look for the move in the smaller party's price.
- **A company leg is company-specific only when its peers moved otherwise.** With the sector beta fixed at 0, the cached peers are the only check. On Monday NVIDIA's +1.68% came while AMD fell 3.61% and INTC 5.67%.
