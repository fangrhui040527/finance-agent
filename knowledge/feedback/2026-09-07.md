## Lessons proposed

- A derived series that silently falls back to an older common observation will
  date a whole page wrongly; the fallback must appear in the output, not only in
  the code. The blank proxy row cost six of nine names their session and nothing
  in the pack said so.
- A thinly traded instrument is a poor market proxy on exactly the days it
  matters, because the sessions it fails to print are not random with respect to
  market activity.
- A capped request budget is a hypothesis about why requests fail, not a fix;
  it needs its own measurement afterwards. Two runs in, the cap has not moved
  the refusal rate it was justified by.
