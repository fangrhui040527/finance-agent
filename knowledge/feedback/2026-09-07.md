## Lessons proposed

- A market proxy that returns exactly +0.00% makes every component's unexplained share 100% by arithmetic, not by discovery. Falsifiable: on any session where the proxy's 1d return is 0.00%, every name's unexplained share on that session is 100% and its market component is 0.00%; if a name ever shows otherwise on such a session, the decomposition is wrong.
- An event's importance and its price impact are different measurements, and the page must report the first even when the second is absent. Falsifiable: Genting's Fitch downgrade on 2026-09-07 coincided with a +0.49% session, 0.39 sigma; if a rating action reliably moved this book's names, the four such actions collected over the next quarter would each fall on a session above 1 sigma.
- A schedule can be failing while every one of its runs succeeds. Falsifiable: every scheduled `collect` run on 2026-09-07 completed with conclusion `success`, and all three were between 5h21m and 5h43m late or absent; a monitor that reads run status or last-run timestamp reports this day as healthy, and only a rule counting owed-against-arrived firings reports it as short.
