## Lessons proposed

- The same session read twice is two different pages, and only the fetch time tells them apart. Apple moved 1.12pp between a pre-close build and a settled one without trading; a page that does not know which cache it was built from does not know what it says.
- A name that is quiet in the corpus and violent in the tape is the case the collector exists for, and the case it handles worst. The largest move in this book's record produced zero rows, on a name that has produced zero rows for three sessions.
- A confirming observation can arrive and still not move the name. Tenaga's AFA review produced the relief the press asked for and the stock printed +0.00%: the market had either priced it or judged it not TNB's cost — and the page cannot tell which without a figure.
- A beta that flips sign from page to page is not a measurement of the market leg; it is a measurement of the window. On such a name the residual is the move, and a page that prints a market component of +0.03% is printing the window, not the market.
- An entity match on one word is not a mention. *Genting Plantations* in a momentum list is not Genting Bhd, and a linker that cannot tell them apart inflates the coverage count of the name it was meant to protect.
