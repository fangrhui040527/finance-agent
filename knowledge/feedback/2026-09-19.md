## Lessons proposed

- A no-session day is a page about the collector, not the market. Ninety-seven Malaysian business rows arrived on a Saturday and none named a book company; the weekend press is where the market is discussed and not where these six names are covered.
- A moves table under a Saturday heading is Friday's table with a date on each row. The pack labels the last session; a page that copies the table without reading the label publishes Thursday's moves as Saturday's, and this one copied the date instead.
- Macro vintages keep their own calendar. August CPI for Malaysia printed while both price series slept, and the first session that can trade it is Monday's; the page records the print on the day it became knowable, not on the day something moves.
