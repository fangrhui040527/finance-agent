## Lessons proposed

- **A results filing settles a calendar dispute.** For Microsoft the fact book held 10-27 and 10-28 from one vendor; the 8-K with Item 2.02 dated 10-28 settled it. The vendor's later listing (10-28, first fetched 10-09) was right and its older one (10-27, from 09-05) was stale. When calendars disagree, wait for the filing rather than treating any one vendor listing as the date.
