## Lessons proposed

- **A company event can arrive without a session to read it.** Apple's verdict was published 3h30m after Friday's close. A page that attributes Monday's move to it has to date the story against Monday's session, not against the day it was published.
- **The row that names a company can arrive a day after the story.** The Supreme Court ruling first reached the corpus during Friday's Bursa session, in a row that named Fortis rather than IHH; the row that named IHH came the next day. Linking by name dates a story by its second telling.
